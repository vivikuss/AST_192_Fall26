DRAFT pre-registration: WASP-discovered HJ hosts vs matched field and vs EBLM IV primaries, project 08
Status: DRAFT for user review, 2026-11-04. NOT approved, NOT frozen, no sha256. Open judgment calls are marked
[Q1]-[Q10] and listed in section G; they are the user's to decide. Source and evidence: DECISIONS.md Stage E
(E1-E5); notes/hu2026_eblm_cuts.md, notes/hu2026_wasp_counts.md, notes/hu2026_power.md.

0. Why, and what is already known (disclosure).
   - Hu, Zhu, Huang & Zhang (2026, arXiv:2608.12790) attribute HS19's cold HJ hosts to survey heterogeneity
     (E1). Our C-2 contrast (EBLMs +7.90 km/s hotter than the HS19 Table 1 hosts, [+2.07, +13.74]) compares a
     single-survey sample (EBLM IV: WASP-South candidates, CORALIE) with that mixed-survey sample. This test
     asks whether the contrast survives when the HJ hosts also come from WASP.
   - Already computed or seen before this draft:
     - the total dispersion (eq. 1) of all 297 Phase 1 hosts (36.18 km/s), which includes the WASP hosts;
     - the EBLM primary result and C-2 (D11) and the exploratory width analysis (D15), which used random 79-host
       subsamples of the 297 (not selected by survey);
     - Hu et al.'s WASP result: 102 hosts within 600 pc, σ_W = 17.7 km/s, < 1σ from their matched field (DR3);
     - counts and field-only power for the WASP subsample (E2-E4).
   - Not computed for any sample in this project: any dispersion of a WASP-only subsample; any σ_W.

1. Competing predictions.
   - HS19 (tidal destruction of HJs during the main sequence): WASP HJ hosts are kinematically colder than
     their matched field, by about the HS19 amount (dispersion ratio 0.845, i.e. −6.7 km/s in the total
     dispersion and about −2.9 km/s in σ_W if every component scales alike), and colder than the EBLM
     primaries, which are field-like (D11).
   - Hu et al. (survey heterogeneity): WASP HJ hosts match their field in σ_W (no offset), and WASP HJ hosts
     and EBLM primaries do not differ. The earlier C-2 contrast then reflects the survey mix of the HS19 sample.

A. Pipeline (as Phase 1 and Stage D unless stated).
   - Gaia [Q1: DR2 as Phase 1, or DR3]. BJ18 distances, STILISM extinction with CV18 coefficients, the
     published MS polynomial, astropy UVW (DECISIONS D9-D16).
   - WASP HJ hosts: the Phase 1 primary sample (HS19 Table 1, 297 hosts), restricted to hosts whose hot Jupiter
     has a WASP discovery facility in the NASA Exoplanet Archive (`disc_facility`, query of 2026-11-04)
     [Q2: facility or name label; Q3: which hemispheres]. Distance cut [Q4]. Colour cut [Q5].
   - EBLM primaries: the 79 of the frozen pre-registration (EBLM IV, γ from the adopted amendment fit, drift
     option (a)), with the same distance and colour cuts as the WASP hosts [Q4, Q5].
   - Field: the Phase 1 field without the host lists and the 118 EBLM IV primaries (D9) [Q6: Hu et al.'s
     halo cut v_tot > 180 km/s or not].
   - Statistics:
     - total dispersion: HS19 eq. 1 as typeset, σ = (1/N) Σ |v_i − v̄| (mean residual speed, D14);
     - vertical dispersion: σ_W = sqrt((1/N) Σ (W_i − W̄)²) (Hu et al. eq. 2; 1/N).

B. Arms. Each arm gives (i) WASP hosts vs matched field and (ii) EBLMs vs matched field under the same
   arm, and (iii) the contrast.
   (a) Total dispersion, HS19-style matching: z-histogram weighting (50 pc fixed bins) plus one field star
       within 0.025 mag in (BP−RP)₀ per host, no reuse within a sample; 40,000 control samples. For the EBLMs,
       the frozen primary's z + distance matching, unchanged.
   (b) σ_W with the same matching as (a).
   (c) σ_W with Hu et al. Table 1 matching: angular separation ≤ 10°; d_HJ − 100 pc ≤ d_FS ≤ d_HJ + 50 pc;
       G_HJ − 1 ≤ G_FS ≤ G_HJ + 0.1; |Δ(BP−RP)₀| ≤ 0.2; one random match per star per iteration, with
       replacement [Q7: 10,000 iterations as Hu et al. or 40,000 as ours]. The EBLMs are matched the same way.
   - Per arm and sample: Δ = statistic(sample) − median(control), with a 95 % percentile CI from 40,000
     bootstrap resamples of the sample (control median fixed), as in the frozen pre-registration B; plus the
     HS19-style fraction of controls as cold or colder.
   - Contrast per arm: Δ_EBLM − Δ_WASP (difference of field offsets, so each sample keeps its own field
     matching), 95 % CI from a joint bootstrap of both samples. For arm (a) only, also the C-2-style direct
     comparison (EBLM bootstrap vs WASP hosts matched within 0.05 mag and the same EBLM distance quintile).
   - Primary result: [Q8: which single arm and which comparison is primary].

C. Expected precision (field velocities only; notes/hu2026_power.md, E4). For 97 WASP hosts (d < 600 pc):
   - vs field, two-sided 5 %, power for an HS19-sized offset: (a) 0.80 (smallest offset at 80 % power,
     6.7 km/s); (b) 0.38 (4.9 km/s, a ratio of 0.74).
   - (c) cannot be estimated with the DR2 field: 21 of 97 hosts have no Hu-style match and the rest have a
     median of 9 (DR2 RVs reach only G ≈ 13, and the G window admits almost no star fainter than the host).
     Hu et al. had about 1,000 matches per host with DR3. The partial estimate (0.46) is an overestimate.
   - Contrast WASP vs EBLM (normal approximation, EBLMs field-like, WASP hosts HS19-cold): total 0.42
     (smallest detectable difference 10.6 km/s); σ_W 0.16 (8.8 km/s).
   - A Dec < 0 WASP subsample (70 hosts, a proxy for South only) lowers power: (a) 0.68, (b) 0.28.

D. What would count as support.
   - For HS19: the WASP hosts' CI lies entirely below 0 in arm (a) and in at least one σ_W arm, and the
     contrast shows the EBLMs hotter than the WASP hosts (CI entirely > 0) in arm (a).
   - For Hu et al.: in the σ_W arms the WASP CI includes 0 and its lower bound lies above the HS19-sized
     offset (≈ −2.9 km/s), so an HS19-sized deficit is excluded; and the contrast CI includes 0. If arm (a)
     alone shows a deficit while (b) and (c) do not, that supports Hu et al.'s point that the total
     dispersion is the less reliable age indicator, not the tidal interpretation.
   - Null (CI includes both 0 and the HS19-sized offset): no claim for either paper. With the power in C, this
     is the likely outcome in the σ_W arms (power ≈ 0.4) and for the contrast (0.16-0.42). A null contrast
     then means that the WASP-only comparison cannot confirm or refute the +7.9 km/s C-2 result; it does not
     show that the EBLMs and WASP hosts are alike. C-2 is reported from then on with the caveat that its HJ
     sample mixes surveys.
   - Mixed (e.g. WASP hosts cold vs field but the contrast null): reported as is, with no claim on the
     contrast.

E. Sensitivity (5,000 iterations each): name-based WASP label (adds WASP-53 and WASP-81); Dec < 0 only;
   without the distance cut; without the colour cut; RMS instead of mean residual speed in arm (a); without
   J1500-33 (as G11); the 0.8 ≤ (BP−RP)₀ ≤ 1.5 cut (12 EBLMs; reported, too small to interpret).

F. Exploratory, labelled as such: σ_U and σ_V separately; HATNet- and HATSouth-only host samples as Hu et al.

G. Open judgment calls for the user (none decided here).
   Q1. Gaia release. DR2 keeps continuity with Phase 1, the frozen EBLM pre-registration and C-2, but cannot
       support arm (c) (C). DR3 is what Hu et al. use and makes (c) feasible, but Phase 2 (DR3) is on hold and
       is being split with a teammate. Options: DR2 for (a, b) and DR3 for (c); all DR3; or drop (c).
   Q2. WASP label: archive `disc_facility` (98 of the 297) or a "WASP-" name (100; adds WASP-53 and WASP-81,
       whose facility is "Multiple Facilities").
   Q3. SuperWASP-North hosts. The archive names a hemisphere for only 16 of the 98 (15 South, 1 North); 82 say
       "SuperWASP". EBLM IV comes from WASP-South. Options: all WASP hosts; Dec < 0 as a proxy (70 within
       600 pc); or a South/North label from each discovery paper (to be compiled).
   Q4. Distance cut: none (98 WASP / 79 EBLM), d < 600 pc as Hu et al. (97 / 71), or matched distance ranges.
   Q5. Colour cut: none, 0.5-1.5 as Hu et al. (94 WASP within 600 pc / 69 EBLM), or 0.8-1.5 (12 EBLMs only).
   Q6. Hu et al.'s field halo cut (v_tot > 180 km/s): apply in arm (c) only, in all arms, or not at all.
   Q7. Iterations and replacement in arm (c): Hu et al.'s 10,000 draws with replacement, or 40,000 as ours.
   Q8. The primary result: which arm ((a) continues our metric; (b)/(c) are Hu et al.'s) and which comparison
       (WASP vs field, or the contrast with the EBLMs). Given the power, also whether to pre-commit to
       treating a null as "no claim".
   Q9. Whether σ_W is also added for the EBLM-vs-field comparison as a reported result (it would be the first
       σ_W computed for the EBLMs), or only inside the contrast.
   Q10. Hu et al. also remove hosts with close stellar companions and hosts in clusters, and apply only
       astrometric cuts 1-3 to hosts. Whether to mirror these for the WASP hosts.
