Amendment to the RV-arm blinding rules of project 07 (07_EBLM_vs_HJ_Tertiaries):
permitted systemic-velocity fits for project 08 (HS19 kinematics)
(specified 2026-10-30, before any orbit fit of any CORALIE RV, and before any RV
value, residual, drift or trend of any EBLM was viewed)

What this amends
- 07 DECISIONS.md, "2026-10-21: New stage - RV arm inventory", section "Blinding":
  no orbit fits, residuals, drifts, trends or RV-vs-time plots before the 07 RV
  pre-registration. 07 has no RV pre-registration yet; these rules stay in force
  for everything not listed below.
- Project 08 needs one quantity from these data: the systemic velocity (gamma) of each
  EBLM IV primary, for Galactic UVW velocities.

Data covered
- The user's CORALIE07 and CORALIE14 curated files (<star>_coralie.rdb), columns jdb,
  vrad and svrad only, for the 118 EBLM IV systems (Triaud et al. 2017,
  arXiv:1707.07521). No other columns and no DRS summary files (resum_*.rdb) are read.
- Data-owner permission is being confirmed by the user. No fit runs before it is.

Permitted computation (per system, nothing else)
1. Fixed inputs, all from EBLM IV: P and the primary-eclipse epoch T0,pri (tab:obs);
   e and omega (tab:params; e = 0 where only an upper limit is given). Where EBLM IV
   selected a drift model, the published lin/quad/cubic coefficients (tab:params) are
   subtracted as fixed terms. Where it selected a two-Keplerian model (tab:triple),
   the published outer orbit is subtracted as a fixed term. Nothing is fitted for them.
2. Instrument offset: one fixed global CORALIE07 -> CORALIE14 offset of 0 m/s for all
   systems. The literature magnitude, |offset| <~ 20 m/s (EBLM IV: "of order 10 m/s";
   Neveu-VanMalle et al. 2016, arXiv:1509.07750, WASP-47: 18 +- 11 m/s, sign not
   stated), is treated as a global systematic: 0.020 km/s is added in quadrature to
   every sigma_gamma. No per-system or per-instrument-era offset is fitted, and no
   quantity is computed or output per instrument era (user decision, 2026-11-01).
3. Model: RV(t) = gamma + K * [cos(nu(t) + omega) + e cos(omega)], with nu from the
   fixed P, T0, e and omega. T0,pri is the primary-eclipse time, and T_peri follows
   from e and omega. For e = 0 this is RV = gamma - K sin(2 pi (t - T0)/P). It is
   linear in (gamma, K) and solved by weighted linear least squares. Weights are
   1/(svrad^2 + sigma_add^2), with sigma_add from EBLM IV tab:obs.
4. sigma_gamma comes from the parameter covariance with these fixed weights, plus the
   0.020 km/s offset systematic in quadrature. It is NOT rescaled by the residual
   scatter or chi^2 (that would expose residual information).
5. Simple average: the unweighted mean of the same RVs, after the same fixed drift,
   outer-orbit and offset corrections.

Permitted outputs (per system; nothing else is saved, printed or viewed)
- gamma [km/s], sigma_gamma [km/s], K [km/s], gamma - (simple average) [km/s].
- A flag if the fit cannot be computed (e.g. fewer than 3 RVs or a singular design).
- Per-system outputs are written only to project 08's gitignored private folder. Only
  aggregates go into reports or git. For example: the distributions of sigma_gamma and
  of gamma - average, the comparison of fitted K with published K, and comparisons
  with Gaia DR3 two-body-orbit gammas and the ~25 published gammas.

Not permitted (until 07's own RV pre-registration)
- Residuals, residual rms or chi^2, per-epoch model values, any drift or trend estimate,
  any per-instrument-era quantity, plots of RV against time or phase, and any other
  statistic computed from the per-epoch RVs.
- The fitting function returns only the four permitted values. Intermediate arrays are
  not logged or saved, and the code is reviewed for this before it is run.

Effect on 07
- 07 already knows the published EBLM IV orbits (P, K, e, omega, drift selections).
  The permitted outputs add gamma and its formal error. gamma - average depends mainly
  on phase coverage and is not a trend measure. No information about unmodelled
  long-term trends is produced. The residual risk is accepted by the user (option b,
  2026-10-30).
