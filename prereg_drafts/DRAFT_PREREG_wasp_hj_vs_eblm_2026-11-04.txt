0. Design principle.
   The primary arm differs from the frozen HS19 pipeline (Phase 1 primary: HS19 Table 1 hosts, Gaia DR2,
   z + colour matching, eq. 1 statistic, 40,000 control samples) in exactly one way: only WASP-discovered
   hosts are kept. This isolates Hu et al.'s sample-heterogeneity argument from their metric argument
   (σ_W, their matching). Every other change is a sensitivity or exploratory arm.

1. Prior knowledge (disclosed before any WASP-only dispersion is computed).
   - Hu, Zhu, Huang & Zhang (2026, arXiv:2608.12790) already report that WASP hosts within 600 pc match the
     field in σ_W: 102 hosts, σ_W = 17.7 km/s, < 1σ from their matched field (Gaia DR3, their own Table 1
     matching). This was known before our test was run.
   - Already computed in this project:
     - the total dispersion (eq. 1) of all 297 Phase 1 hosts, 36.18 km/s vs a matched-field median of
       42.82 km/s; the WASP hosts are among the 297, but no WASP-only value has been computed;
     - the EBLM primary result (Δσ = +0.50 km/s, 95 % CI [−4.23, +4.71]) and C-2 (+7.90 km/s,
       [+2.07, +13.74]), D11;
     - the exploratory width analysis (D15), which used random 79-host subsamples of the 297, not selected by
       survey;
     - counts and field-only power for the WASP subsample (E2-E4).
   - Not computed for any sample: a WASP-only dispersion; any σ_W of hosts or EBLMs.

2. Competing predictions.
   - HS19 (tidal destruction during the main sequence): WASP hosts are colder than their matched field by
     about the HS19 amount. "HS19-sized" is defined as every velocity scaled by 36.18/42.82 = 0.845, i.e.
     δ_HS19 = −(1 − 0.845) × median(control) ≈ −6.7 km/s in the total dispersion (≈ −2.9 km/s in σ_W if every
     component scales alike). The EBLMs, which are field-like (D11), are then hotter than the WASP hosts.
   - Hu et al. (survey heterogeneity): WASP hosts match their field, and WASP hosts and EBLMs do not differ;
     the earlier C-2 contrast reflects the survey mix of the HS19 Table 1 sample.

A. Pipeline. Identical to the frozen Phase 1 pipeline unless stated (DECISIONS D1-D20).
   - Gaia DR2, cuts 1-4 for hosts, BJ18 distances, STILISM extinction with CV18 coefficients, the published
     MS polynomial (1 mag cut), astropy UVW, host RVs as Phase 1.
   - Statistic: HS19 eq. 1 as typeset, σ = (1/N) Σ |v_i − v̄| (mean residual speed; D14).
   - Field: the Phase 1 field (host lists removed, D17).
   - Matching: z-histogram thinning (50 pc fixed bins) and one field star within 0.025 mag in (BP−RP)₀ per
     host, no reuse within a sample; hosts without a match in an iteration are dropped from both sides
     (D13b). 40,000 control samples, seeds as D19.
   - WASP hosts: the Phase 1 primary sample (297) restricted to hosts whose hot Jupiter has a WASP
     facility in the NASA Exoplanet Archive `disc_facility` ("SuperWASP", "SuperWASP-South", "WASP-South",
     "SuperWASP-North"; pscomppars query of 2026-11-04, cached): **98 hosts**. South and North are both
     included. No distance cut and no new colour cut.
   - EBLM primaries (for the secondary result): the 79 of the frozen pre-registration
     (`PREREG_eblm_kinematics_2026-11-02.txt`), with its γ, field (D9) and z + distance matching, unchanged.

B. PRIMARY RESULT: WASP hosts vs matched field, total dispersion.
   - Δ_W = σ_WASP − median(σ_control).
   - 95 % CI: percentile interval over 40,000 bootstrap resamples of the WASP hosts, control median fixed;
     bias reported, BCa alongside if |bias| > 0.2 × the half-width (as the frozen pre-registration B).
   - Also reported: the HS19-style fraction of controls with σ_control ≤ σ_WASP (paired as D13b).
   - Expected precision (field only, E4): control SD 2.55 km/s for 98 hosts; power 0.81 for an HS19-sized
     deficit (two-sided 5 %); smallest deficit detected with 80 % power 6.6 km/s.
   - Decision rule (δ_HS19 computed from this run's control median, ≈ −6.7 km/s):
     - **Supports HS19:** the whole CI is < 0. If in addition the CI's lower bound is above δ_HS19, the
       deficit is reported as real but smaller than HS19's.
     - **Supports Hu et al.:** the CI includes 0 and its lower bound is above δ_HS19, so an HS19-sized
       deficit is excluded.
     - **Inconclusive:** the CI includes both 0 and δ_HS19. No claim.
     - **Unexpected:** the whole CI is > 0 (hotter than the field). Predicted by neither; reported, no claim.

C. SECONDARY RESULT: WASP hosts vs EBLM primaries, total dispersion.
   - Contrast = Δ_EBLM − Δ_W, the difference of the two field offsets (each sample with its own matching:
     z for the WASP hosts as Phase 1, z + distance for the EBLMs as the frozen EBLM primary). 95 % CI from
     40,000 joint bootstrap resamples of both samples, each control median fixed.
   - Stated in advance: power ≈ 0.42 for an HS19-sized contrast (WASP hosts HS19-cold, EBLMs field-like;
     normal approximation from field-control SDs 2.55 and 2.79 km/s); smallest contrast detected with 80 %
     power ≈ 10.6 km/s.
   - Reading: HS19 predicts a contrast of about +6.7 km/s; Hu et al. predict 0. The whole CI > 0 supports an
     HJ-specific deficit among WASP hosts. A CI that includes 0 is inconclusive at this power: it neither
     confirms nor refutes the C-2 result (+7.9 km/s), and C-2 is reported from then on with the caveat that
     its HJ sample mixes surveys.

D. Sensitivity (5,000 control samples and 5,000 bootstrap resamples each, as the frozen G tests; each
   changes one thing relative to B and C):
   1. Membership by name: add hosts named WASP whose archive facility is not WASP (WASP-53, WASP-81; 100).
   2. Distance cut d < 600 pc (BJ18), applied to the WASP hosts in B and to both the WASP hosts and the EBLMs
      in C (97 WASP hosts, 71 EBLMs).
   3. Colour cut 0.5 ≤ (BP−RP)₀ ≤ 1.5, applied in the same way (95 WASP hosts;
      76 EBLMs). The 0.8-1.5 cut is not used: only 12 EBLMs survive it.
   4. Halo cut: field stars with v_tot = sqrt(U² + V² + W²) > 180 km/s removed (Hu et al.). It is applied to
      field stars only and trims the field's velocity tail, so it is expected to lower the control dispersions and
      so to make the hosts look less cold.
   5. Hosts with close stellar companions or in clusters removed (Hu et al.'s exclusions). EBLMs are
      binaries by definition, so a companion-removal rule for the HJ hosts needs justification before it is
      read as making the samples more alike; it is reported for comparison with Hu et al. only.
   Carried over unchanged from the frozen EBLM pre-registration, for C only: without J1500-33 (G11).

E. EXPLORATORY: σ_W. All σ_W results are exploratory.
   - σ_W = sqrt((1/N) Σ (W_i − W̄)²) (Hu et al. eq. 2), with the matching of A (not Hu et al.'s matching).
   - (i) WASP hosts vs field: power ≈ 0.36 for an HS19-sized deficit (98 hosts; 0.38 for 97 within 600 pc).
   - (ii) WASP vs EBLM contrast in σ_W: power ≈ 0.15-0.16.
   - (iii) The EBLMs' σ_W on its own, with a 95 % bootstrap CI, and its offset from the matched field.
   - **A null in σ_W is declared uninformative in advance.** Only a CI that excludes 0 is reported as a
     finding, and it remains exploratory.

F. EXPLORATORY: WASP-South only, with each host's hemisphere taken from its discovery paper (the archive's
   `disc_refname`), done only if a hemisphere can be assigned to every one of the 98 hosts. No declination
   proxy is used. If any host cannot be assigned, the arm is dropped and the reason recorded.

G. Not in this pre-registration: Hu et al.'s Table 1 matching (angular separation, distance and G windows).
   It needs a field reaching fainter than the DR2 RV limit (E4) and moves to Phase 2 (Gaia DR3).

H. Scope. Conclusions stay within the stated precision. Anything not listed here is labelled exploratory.
