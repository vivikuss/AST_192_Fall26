1. What went wrong.
   - Section A defines the WASP hosts as the Phase 1 primary sample (297) "restricted to hosts whose hot
     Jupiter has a WASP facility in the NASA Exoplanet Archive disc_facility". It gives 98 hosts.
   - The code (src/hu2026_checks.py wasp_hosts, as of commit 898b81a) attached the facility by host
     name. For HS19 Table 1 rows, the name column holds HS19's SimbadName, but the facility table is keyed
     by the archive's host name. A host whose SimbadName is a catalogue designation therefore got no
     label, even though its hot Jupiter is a WASP discovery. The name-based variant (D1) had the same
     fault.
   - Nine hosts in the 297 were affected. Matched on the Gaia DR2 source_id instead, they are:

     | Archive name | HS19 SimbadName | disc_facility | disc_year |

3. The only change: the membership join, plus the items in sections 4-8.
   - **Join.** WASP membership is assigned by matching the host's Gaia DR2 source_id to the archive's
     gaia_dr2_id (code: wasp_hosts(cfg, join="source_id")).
   - **Planets considered (Q1).** The facility is taken from the host's planets in the archive's
     hot-Jupiter selection: pscomppars snapshot 2026-10-28, P < 10 d, pl_bmassj > 0.1 MJup with a measured
     mass provenance (upper limits kept), **and disc_year ≤ 2019**. Facilities come from the 2026-11-04
     disc_facility query.
     - The frozen run used this selection, but the frozen text did not state it. It is stated here.
     - The year limit excludes **none** of the 107 hosts. The count is 107 with the year limit, without
       it, or when every planet of the host is considered.
   - **Name-based variant D1 (Q4).** The name test uses HS19's ArchiveName ("WASP-" prefix), not the
     SimbadName: **109 hosts** (107 plus WASP-53 and WASP-81, whose facility is "Multiple Facilities").
     - Why 109 and 107 differ (notes/amendment1_join_audit.md; metadata only). The name set has two hosts the
       source_id set lacks; the source_id set has none that the name set lacks.

       | Host | DR2 source_id, source_id route | DR2 source_id, name route | Separation | G | Parallax (mas) |
       |---|---|---|---|---|---|
       | WASP-53 | 5136559128264765184 | 5136559128264765184 | 0″ (same source) | 12.35 | 4.938 ± 0.034 |
       | WASP-81 | 4244243354278985472 | 4244243354278985472 | 0″ (same source) | 12.33 | 2.482 ± 0.044 |

     - Class: "other". Neither is a wrong-source match, a missing source_id or a duplicate. Both routes
       reach the same Gaia DR2 star. The archive gives their hot Jupiters the disc_facility "Multiple
       Facilities", so the facility test (A) excludes them and the name test (D1) includes them. The
       difference comes from the definitions, not from the join, and the 107-host sample is unaffected.
     - Over all 297 hosts, the pipeline source_id equals the archive gaia_dr2_id reached by ArchiveName for
       296. The exception is HATS-67 (WASP by neither route; DECISIONS D6b). No source_id is duplicated.
     - The 98 → 107 change (section 1) is a different comparison: the 9 hosts had no facility label under
       the SimbadName key. For each of them, both routes give the same source_id (0″), with G 10.9-12.6 and
       a parallax.
   - The facility test and everything else in sections A-E of the frozen text are unchanged. Corrected
     counts:

     | Sample | Frozen run | Corrected |
     |---|---|---|
     | A / B: WASP hosts (disc_facility) | 98 | **107** |
     | D1: plus a name starting "WASP-" (HS19 ArchiveName) | 100 | **109** |

7. Stated power for the corrected membership (Q5).
   - Computed on 2026-11-09 with `python -m src.hu2026_checks power_a1`
     (notes/wasp_amendment1_power.md; data/processed/hu2026/power_amendment1.json).
   - Method as E4: field velocities only, HS19-style z + colour matching, 10,000 control samples; two-sided
     5 %, HS19-sized deficit = ratio 0.845. The contrast uses the normal approximation with the EBLMs'
     field-control SD.
   - **Check:** the faulty 98-host list, recomputed with the original E4 label, reproduces the frozen
     numbers (0.81, 0.36, contrast 0.42).

     | Arm | 98 hosts (frozen) | 107 hosts (corrected) | Smallest offset at 80 % power, 107 |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | B: total, WASP vs field | 0.81 (control SD 2.55) | **0.85** (control SD 2.46, median 43.11) | 6.25 km/s (= 80 % power) |
     | C: contrast, total | 0.42 | **0.43** (SD of difference 3.76) | 10.53 km/s |
     | E(i): σ_W, WASP vs field | 0.36 | **0.38** (control SD 2.07, median 19.07) | 4.80 km/s |
     | E(ii): contrast, σ_W | 0.15-0.16 | **0.16** | 8.70 km/s |

   - **6.25 km/s** is the smallest offset with at least **80 % power** for B (107 hosts; two-sided 5 %;
     field-only; HS19 ratio model; ratio grid step 0.001, about 0.04 km/s). The 98-host offset from the
     frozen run (Δ_W = −5.81 km/s, section 2) is smaller in size than 6.25 km/s.
   - The D3 subset (106, d < 600 pc) gives 0.84 / 0.42 / 0.37 / 0.16.
   - **EBLM resampling behind the contrast rows (C, E(ii)).** The two columns use different Monte Carlo
     draws of the EBLM field controls (10,000 each):
     - frozen column: label hu_contrast_eblm (E4), SD 2.79 km/s total and 2.29 km/s σ_W;
     - corrected column: label a1_contrast_eblm, SD 2.85 km/s and 2.32 km/s.
     - The Monte Carlo SD of each of these SDs is about 0.020 km/s (total) and 0.016 km/s (σ_W). This is
       the normal approximation s/√(2(N−1)) with N = 10,000. The draws themselves were not stored.
     - The differences (0.055 and 0.032 km/s) are about 2.0 and 1.4 times the Monte Carlo SD of a
       difference.
     - Their effect on the contrast power is below 0.01. Under the same EBLM draws (a1_contrast_eblm), the
       98-host list gives 0.42 (0.4165) and the 107-host list 0.43 (0.4281).
     - The frozen column's contrast value (0.42) is E4's d < 600 pc row (97 hosts); E4 has no
       all-distance contrast row.
   - **Reading:** as in the frozen text, a null in σ_W, or in the contrast, is uninformative.
