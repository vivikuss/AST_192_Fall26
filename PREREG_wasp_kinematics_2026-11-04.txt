0. Design principle.
   The primary arm differs from the frozen HS19 pipeline (Phase 1 primary: HS19 Table 1 hosts, Gaia DR2,
   z + colour matching, eq. 1 statistic, 40,000 control samples) in exactly one way: only WASP-discovered
   hosts are kept. This isolates Hu et al.'s sample-heterogeneity argument from their metric argument
   (σ_W, their matching). Every other change is a sensitivity or exploratory arm.

1. Prior knowledge (disclosed before any WASP-only dispersion is computed).
   - Hu, Zhu, Huang & Zhang (2026, arXiv:2608.12790) already report that WASP hosts within 600 pc match the
     field in σ_W: 102 hosts, σ_W = 17.7 km/s, < 1σ from their matched field (Gaia DR3, their own Table 1
     matching). This was known before our test was run.
   - Already computed in this project:
     - the total dispersion (eq. 1) of all 297 Phase 1 hosts and its matched field (below); the WASP hosts
       are among the 297, but no WASP-only value has been computed;
     - the EBLM primary result (Δσ = +0.50 km/s, 95 % CI [−4.23, +4.71]) and C-2 (+7.90 km/s,
       [+2.07, +13.74]), D11;
     - the exploratory width analysis (D15), which used random 79-host subsamples of the 297, not selected by
       survey;
     - counts, field-only power and discovery-paper hemisphere labels for the WASP hosts (E2-E4, E22).
   - Not computed for any sample: a WASP-only dispersion; any σ_W of hosts or EBLMs.

2. The HS19-sized offset, δ_HS19.
   - **δ_HS19 = −6.64 km/s** = 36.18 − 42.82 km/s: the Phase 1 primary result (HS19 Table 1 hosts, 297,
     approved at Checkpoint 1), host dispersion 36.1825 km/s minus matched-field median 42.8234 km/s.
     File: `data/processed/phase1_dr2/summary.json`, block `mc.hj_table1` (`host_dispersion`,
     `control_median`); reported in REPRODUCTION_REPORT.md.
   - The frozen EBLM pre-registration quotes the same number rounded to −6.6 km/s. Revision 2 of this draft
     wrote "≈ −6.7 km/s": that was the HS19 ratio 36.18/42.82 = 0.845 applied to the WASP hosts' expected
     control median (43.10 km/s from the field-only power run, E4), i.e. −6.68 km/s. That definition depends
     on this test's own controls; it is not used. δ_HS19 is the fixed value −6.64 km/s.
   - The power figures below were computed for the 0.845 ratio (−6.68 km/s at the WASP control median); for
     a 0.04 km/s difference in effect size they change by less than 0.01.

3. Competing predictions.
   - HS19 (tidal destruction during the main sequence): WASP hosts are colder than their matched field by
     about δ_HS19, and so colder than the EBLMs, which are field-like (D11).
   - Hu et al. (survey heterogeneity): WASP hosts match their field, and WASP hosts and EBLMs do not differ;
     the earlier C-2 contrast reflects the survey mix of the HS19 Table 1 sample.

A. Pipeline. Identical to the frozen Phase 1 pipeline unless stated (DECISIONS D1-D20).
   - Gaia DR2, cuts 1-4 for hosts, BJ18 distances, STILISM extinction with CV18 coefficients, the published
     MS polynomial (1 mag cut), astropy UVW, host RVs as Phase 1.
   - Statistic: HS19 eq. 1 as typeset, σ = (1/N) Σ |v_i − v̄| (mean residual speed; D14).
   - Field: the Phase 1 field (host lists removed, D17).
   - Matching: z-histogram thinning (50 pc fixed bins) and one field star within 0.025 mag in (BP−RP)₀ per
     host, no reuse within a sample; hosts without a match in an iteration are dropped from both sides
     (D13b). 40,000 control samples, seeds as D19.
   - WASP hosts: the Phase 1 primary sample (297) restricted to hosts whose hot Jupiter has a WASP
     facility in the NASA Exoplanet Archive `disc_facility` ("SuperWASP", "SuperWASP-South", "WASP-South",
     "SuperWASP-North"; pscomppars query of 2026-11-04, cached): **98 hosts**, South and North. No distance
     cut and no new colour cut. This and the other WASP-host counts in this draft (100, 97, 95) were
     computed by us from the Phase 1 sample (E3, E24). Hu et al. count 102 WASP hosts within 600 pc; their
     number differs because they use Gaia DR3 (distances and RVs) and their own selection (their updated
     HS19 list, exclusions and cuts).
   - EBLM primaries (for the secondary result): the 79 of the frozen pre-registration
     (`PREREG_eblm_kinematics_2026-11-02.txt`), with its γ, field (D9) and z + distance matching, unchanged.

B. PRIMARY RESULT: WASP hosts vs matched field, total dispersion.
   - Δ_W = σ_WASP − median(σ_control).
   - 95 % CI [L, U]: percentile interval over 40,000 bootstrap resamples of the WASP hosts, control median
     fixed; bias reported, BCa alongside if |bias| > 0.2 × the half-width. This is the interval type of the
     frozen EBLM pre-registration (`PREREG_eblm_kinematics_2026-11-02.txt`, section B: "percentile interval
     over 40,000 bootstrap resamples ... the percentile interval stays primary"). The decision rule uses the
     percentile interval; a BCa interval, if reported, does not change the case.
   - Also reported: the HS19-style fraction of controls with σ_control ≤ σ_WASP (paired as D13b).
   - Expected precision (field only, E4): control SD 2.55 km/s for 98 hosts; power 0.81 for an HS19-sized
     deficit (two-sided 5 %); smallest deficit detected with 80 % power 6.6 km/s.
   - Decision rule. δ = δ_HS19 = −6.64 km/s. "Contains x" means L ≤ x ≤ U. Exactly one case applies:

     | Case | Condition on [L, U] | Reading |
     |---|---|---|
     | 1 | U < 0 and L ≤ δ ≤ U | Supports HS19: a deficit consistent with HS19's size. |
     | 2 | U < δ | A cold offset larger than HS19's (supports HS19's direction; the size is not HS19's). |
     | 3 | δ < L and U < 0 | A real but smaller deficit: partial support for HS19; heterogeneity explains part of the effect. |
     | 4 | L ≤ 0 ≤ U and L > δ | Supports Hu et al.: no deficit, and an HS19-sized one is excluded. |
     | 5 | L ≤ δ and U ≥ 0 | Inconclusive: no claim. |
     | 6 | L > 0 | Hotter than the field: predicted by neither paper; reported, no claim. |

     Cases 1 and 2 stay separate, but both count against the survey-mixing explanation: a single-survey
     sample that is still as cold as, or colder than, the mixed HS19 sample is not explained by mixing surveys.

     The cases cover every interval and do not overlap: an interval either lies wholly above 0 (6), wholly
     below δ (2), or meets [δ, 0]; in the last case it contains both ends (5), only δ (1), only 0 (4) or
     neither (3).

C. SECONDARY RESULT: WASP hosts vs EBLM primaries, total dispersion.
   - Contrast = Δ_EBLM − Δ_W, the difference of the two field offsets. Each half keeps its frozen matching:
     z only for the WASP hosts (Phase 1), z + distance for the EBLMs (frozen EBLM primary). 95 % CI from
     40,000 joint bootstrap resamples of both samples, each control median fixed.
   - Stated in advance: power ≈ 0.42 for an HS19-sized contrast (WASP hosts HS19-cold, EBLMs field-like;
     normal approximation from field-control SDs 2.55 and 2.79 km/s); smallest contrast detected with 80 %
     power ≈ 10.6 km/s.
   - Reading: HS19 predicts a contrast of about +6.64 km/s; Hu et al. predict 0. The whole CI > 0 supports an
     HJ-specific deficit among WASP hosts. A CI that includes 0 is inconclusive at this power: it neither
     confirms nor refutes the C-2 result (+7.9 km/s), and C-2 is reported from then on with the caveat that
     its HJ sample mixes surveys. The whole CI < 0 (WASP hosts hotter than the EBLMs, relative to their
     fields) is predicted by neither paper; reported, no claim.
   - Limitation: the two halves are matched differently (z only vs z + distance), so part of the contrast
     could come from the matching rather than the samples. Distance matching changed the EBLM control
     width by only 0.03-0.10 km/s (D15), but its effect on the control median of the WASP hosts is not known.
     Sensitivity D2 below matches the WASP hosts the same way.

D. Sensitivity (5,000 control samples and 5,000 bootstrap resamples each, as the frozen G tests; each
   changes one thing relative to B and C):
   1. Membership by name: add hosts named WASP whose archive facility is not WASP (WASP-53, WASP-81; 100,
      computed by us).
   2. WASP hosts matched on z and distance (5 quantile bins of the WASP hosts' own distances), as the EBLMs
      are; B and C rerun with it. Each sample uses its own distance quintiles because each is matched to
      its own field in the same way: the frozen EBLM primary bins the field by the EBLMs' distance
      quintiles, so the WASP hosts' field is binned by the WASP hosts' quintiles.
   3. Distance cut d < 600 pc (BJ18), applied to the WASP hosts in B and to both the WASP hosts and the EBLMs
      in C (97 WASP hosts, 71 EBLMs).
   4. Colour cut 0.5 ≤ (BP−RP)₀ ≤ 1.5, applied in the same way (95 WASP hosts, computed by us; 76 EBLMs). The 0.8-1.5 cut is
      not used: only 12 EBLMs survive it.
   5. Halo cut: field stars with v_tot = sqrt(U² + V² + W²) > 180 km/s removed (Hu et al.). It is applied to
      field stars only and trims the field's velocity tail, so it is expected to lower the control
      dispersions and so to make the hosts look less cold.
   6. C only: without J1500-33. This is the EBLM analysis's test G11, added by the user after STOP D1 and
      before any EBLM kinematics (DECISIONS D8). It is not in the frozen EBLM pre-registration file; it is
      kept here because the same EBLM sample is used.

E. EXPLORATORY: σ_W. All σ_W results are exploratory.
   - σ_W = sqrt((1/N) Σ (W_i − W̄)²) (Hu et al. eq. 2), with the matching of A (not Hu et al.'s matching).
   - (i) WASP hosts vs field: power ≈ 0.36 for an HS19-sized deficit (98 hosts; 0.38 for 97 within 600 pc).
     For σ_W the HS19-sized deficit is defined by the ratio, −(1 − 0.845) × median(σ_W control) (≈ −2.9 km/s).
     This differs from the fixed −6.64 km/s used for the total dispersion because no frozen σ_W result exists
     to take a fixed offset from.
   - (ii) WASP vs EBLM contrast in σ_W: power ≈ 0.15-0.16.
   - (iii) The EBLMs' σ_W on its own, with a 95 % bootstrap CI, and its offset from the matched field.
   - **A null in σ_W is declared uninformative in advance.** Only a CI that excludes 0 is reported as a
     finding, and it remains exploratory.

F. Not in this pre-registration.
   - **WASP-South only.** Dropped: hemisphere labels from the discovery papers (as listed in the archive)
     could not be assigned to every host (E22; notes/wasp_hemisphere_labels.csv). 94 of 98 were assigned
     (59 WASP-South only, 19 SuperWASP-North only, 16 both). For WASP-35, WASP-48, WASP-57 and WASP-127 the
     papers do not say which instrument observed the host. No declination proxy is used.
   - **Hu et al.'s Table 1 matching** (angular separation, distance and G windows): needs a field reaching
     fainter than the DR2 RV limit (E4); moves to Phase 2 (Gaia DR3).
   - **Removing hosts with close stellar companions or in clusters:** Hu et al.'s list of these hosts is not
     published; moves to Phase 2. (EBLMs are binaries by definition, so such a rule for the HJ hosts would
     need justification.)

G. Scope. Conclusions stay within the stated precision. Anything not listed here is labelled exploratory.
