**Hu et al. matching in our DR2 field is not usable as is.** Our field needs a DR2 RV (G ≲ 13), and
the G window (G_HJ − 1 to G_HJ + 0.1) admits almost no field star that is fainter than the host. Hu et al.
report ~1,000 matches per host with DR3. Arm (c) rows above use only the hosts with a match (N shown),
drawing with replacement from ~10 stars, so their control SD is underestimated and the power overstated.

- WASP, all distances: 21 of 98 hosts have no Hu-style match; median 9.5 matches per host.
- WASP, d < 600 pc: 21 of 97 hosts have no Hu-style match; median 9.0 matches per host.
- WASP, d < 600 pc, 0.5-1.5: 20 of 94 hosts have no Hu-style match; median 9.5 matches per host.
- WASP, d < 600 pc, Dec < 0 (proxy for a South-only sample): 16 of 70 hosts have no Hu-style match; median 9.5 matches per host.
